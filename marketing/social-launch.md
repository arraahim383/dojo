# Daba Dojo — early Android test post

## Short version

I built Daba Dojo to make chess practice feel clear and manageable: learn one idea, try it on the board, and see what to work on next.

The Android test build includes interactive lessons, 20,970 offline puzzles, practice bots, endgame drills, and Stockfish game review.

This is an early test release. Progress in this build stays on the device. If you try it, I’d love to hear what felt useful and what felt confusing.

Download the APK: https://dojo-omega-two.vercel.app/downloads/Daba-Dojo.apk

## Longer version

Meet Daba Dojo — a personal chess trainer built around learning, not just playing more games.

Pick up a short interactive lesson, practice the idea with a puzzle, then review a game with Stockfish to understand what happened. There’s also a simple daily plan, endgame drills, opening practice, and adjustable-strength bots.

The Android test build works offline for lessons, puzzles, bots, drills, and game review. Your progress stays on your device in this preview. Account sync is being connected and is not enabled in this APK yet.

This is an early test release, and honest feedback is welcome. What helped you learn? What made you stop and think, “where do I go next?”

Download: https://dojo-omega-two.vercel.app/downloads/Daba-Dojo.apk
